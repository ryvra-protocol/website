# Ryvra Tokenomics FAQ

**As of:** 2026-09-12

> Informational only. Not investment advice, not an offer or solicitation, and not a guarantee of token issuance, value, or return.

## Who is this for?

Users, merchants, developers, and researchers who need a plain-language tokenomics overview before the deeper specification.

## Frequently asked questions

### [User, Merchant, Developer] What is the token used for?

Public materials position the token around future network participation, ecosystem alignment, and governance. Final utility terms are not yet published in binding form.

### [User, Merchant] How do incentives align with network usage?

The clearest public example today is Proof of Transaction, where eligible activity can earn contribution points before any token generation event.

### [User, Developer] What is public today about supply or allocation?

Ryvra has shared illustrative allocation envelope ranges, but it has not published a final supply model or final allocation commitments in binding form.

### [User, Merchant, Developer] Are emissions, vesting, or unlock schedules final?

No. Final emissions, vesting, and unlock mechanics are not public final terms today.

### [Merchant, Developer] What role does governance play, and what are its limits?

Governance may set or approve parameters such as cadence, thresholds, allocation inputs, and future token mechanics, but it does not erase legal, compliance, or publication constraints.

### [User, Merchant, Developer] What key risks should readers keep in mind?

Token timing can change, participation may vary by jurisdiction, and points are not tokens today.
