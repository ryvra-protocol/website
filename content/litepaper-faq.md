# Ryvra Litepaper FAQ

**As of:** 2026-09-12

> Audience-first mirror of the live docs page at `/docs/tokenomics/litepaper-faq`.

## Who is this for?

Users, merchants, developers, and reviewers who want the plain-language trust-model explanation before reading the deeper specification pages.

## Frequently asked questions

### [User, Merchant, Developer] What problem does Ryvra solve?

Ryvra solves the gap between software that can suggest financial actions and systems that must still decide, in a governed way, whether those actions should be allowed to happen.

### [User, Merchant, Developer] What is the difference between AI reasoning and financial authority?

AI reasoning can generate an idea or recommendation. Financial authority is the governed power to approve and execute that idea. Ryvra keeps those two roles separate.

### [Merchant, Developer] How do mandates, policies, and risk approvals work together?

Mandates define scope, policies define the rules, and independent risk decides whether a valid-looking request is still safe enough to proceed.

### [Merchant, Developer] Why does gateway-only execution matter?

Gateway-only execution means agents do not talk directly to money-moving systems. Requests must pass through the governed intake boundary first.

### [User, Merchant, Developer] What does provenance mean for trust?

Provenance is the evidence chain that shows what was proposed, who approved it, what executed, what settled, and whether anyone intervened.
